# Operating Agreement of Pando Collective LLC

This Operating Agreement, dated **September 20th, 2024**, with an effective date of **September 20th, 2024**, by and among the undersigned parties, who by their execution of this Operating Agreement have become members of Pando Collective LLC, a Colorado limited liability company, provided as is stated in this agreement.

## 1. Recitals and Company Formation

1. **_Formation._** The members have formed a Limited Liability Company ( "Pando") according to the laws of the state in which Pando was formed. This operating agreement is entered into and effective as of the date it is adopted by the members.

2. **_Intent._** The undersigned parties have organized Pando as a limited liability company under the laws of the state of Colorado, and wish to enter into this Operating Agreement to set forth the terms and conditions with which the management, business and financial affairs of Pando shall be conducted.

3. **_Collective Goals and Cooperative Principles._** The parties commit to governing Pando as a cooperative entity. In the spirit of cooperative ownership, this agreement underlines our collective principles:
    1. To provide and maintain collective housing, nurturing a community among members.

    2. To educate members on the principles and responsibilities of cooperative property ownership.

    3. To own, operate, and manage Pando's properties cooperatively for the mutual benefit of members.

    4. To responsibly borrow and lend funds, securing them through appropriate legal instruments, in support of Pando's goals and cooperative principles.

    5. To engage in activities, contracts, and endeavors that align with Pando's goals and cooperative principles.

    6. To uphold democratic member control, ensuring operations reflect the collective will and benefit of all members.

4. **_Agreement._** This agreement is based on the mutual commitments and promises made by the parties. Each party acknowledges that they have fully understood and accepted these terms. Based on these mutual understandings, the parties agree to follow and uphold everything stated in this agreement.

5. **_Term._** Pando will continue perpetually unless:
    1. **_Dissolution Procedures_**. Pando dissolves in accordance with the circumstances and procedures outlined in this agreement.
    2. **_Mandated by State Law_**. Any other event that leads to the dissolution of Pando as mandated by the applicable state laws.

## 4. Members

1. **_Class A._** The composition, rights, and obligations of Class A members are defined as follows:
    1. **_Initial Issuance._** Each founding member, listed above, shall be granted Class A membership.

    2. **_Additional Issuance._** New memberships may only be created and granted by a unanimous vote of the existing Class A members.

    3. **_Transferability_**: Class A memberships are non-transferable and cannot be sold or inherited.

    4. **_Expulsion._** A Class A member may be expelled through a unanimous vote of existing members, excluding the member in question. Expulsion results in the exclusive loss of Class A membership. No other membership classes or membership interests are affected.

    5. **_Obligations._** Class A members are obligated to contribute funds for operating expenses, including mortgage payments, Pando property taxes, insurance, and any other routine maintenance costs. The exact breakdown of these costs will vary and will not necessarily be equally split among Class A Members.

    6. **_Voting Power._** Class A membership votes are conducted on a one-person, one-vote basis, otherwise known as per-capita voting.

    7. **_Voting Rights._** Class A members are entitled to vote on board appointments, Pando dissolution, Pando property lease allocations, and all financial transactions that the Board of Directors is not specifically empowered to approve according to the terms of this agreement.

    8. **_Class A Subclasses._** Each Class A member shall be designated as either an Occupant or Non-Occupant Class A member, based on their residential status in relation to Pando's properties.
       1. **_Subclass Voting._** Certain voting matters, as specified in this agreement, may be limited to either Occupant or Non-Occupant Class A members. These exclusive voting rights will be exercised only in specific situations for which the interests or responsibilities of Occupant and Non-Occupant members distinctly differ.

       2. **_Subclass Voting Power._** When voting is limited to a subclass, each member’s vote within that subclass adheres to the general one-person, one-vote principle established for Class A members.

2. **Class B.** The composition, rights, and obligations of Class B members are defined as follows:
    1. **_Issuance._** Class B membership is automatically granted to any individual who owns one or more units of preferred membership interest. Preferred membership units are issued to individuals when they make contributions to Pando, as outlined in Section 6.

    2. **_Transferability_**: Class B memberships are intrinsically tied to the underlying preferred membership interest and shall transfer automatically with it. These memberships cannot be bought, sold, or transferred independently of the associated preferred membership interest.

    3. **_Dissolution._** A Person shall cease to be a Class B Member at such time as they no longer own any preferred membership interest.

    4. **_Voting Power._** The voting power of each Class B Member shall be directly proportional to the number of units of preferred membership interest held by that member.

    5. **_Voting Rights._** Class B members are exclusively entitled to vote on the purchase of new real estate property, the sale of Pando real estate property owned by Pando, Pando dissolution, and certain amendments to this agreement as outlined in this agreement.

3. **Class C.** The composition, rights, and obligations of Class C members are defined as follows:
    1. **_Issuance._** Class C membership is automatically granted to any individual who owns one or more units of common membership interest. Common membership units are issued to individuals when they make contributions to Pando, as outlined in Section 6.

    2. **_Transferability_**: Class C memberships are intrinsically tied to the underlying common membership interest and shall transfer automatically with it. These memberships cannot be bought, sold, or transferred independently of the associated preferred membership interest.

    3. **_Dissolution._** A Person shall cease to be a Class C Member at such time as they no longer own any common Membership Interest.

    4. **_Voting Power._** The voting power of each Class C Member shall be directly proportional to the number of units of common membership interest held by that member.

    5. **_Voting Rights._** Class C members are exclusively entitled to vote on the purchase of new real estate property, the sale of Pando real estate property owned by Pando, Pando dissolution, and certain amendments to this agreement as outlined in this agreement.

4. **_Member Liability_**. The liability of the members will be limited according to state law. No member is an agent of any other member of Pando, solely by reason of being a member.

## 5. Management (Board of Directors)

1. **_Board Selection and Election._** Board members are elected by a simple majority vote of Class A members.
    1. **_Current Board._** The current board of directors at the time of adoption is defined by Exhibit A of this agreement.

    2. **_Elections._** Board elections will be held two (2) months prior to every annual meeting for which there is a director whose term is expiring. This will be done in a regular meeting if there is to be one in the allotted time frame, or a special meeting if there will not be a regular meeting. These meetings will follow the processes and procedures for their respective meeting types, and voting will be as per the rules set forth in this operating agreement.

    3. **_Transition Period._** Once election results have been determined, the incumbent directors and the directors-elect will be beholden to the understanding that the two months between the election meeting and annual meeting are to ensure a smooth and peaceful transition of power. This transition will include all necessary onboarding and training from the outgoing officers to the officers-elect. During the transition period, the board-elect does not have any authority and is not empowered to act as the board.

    4. **_Assumption of Power._** The board-elect will officially assume power and be granted all Board powers after the annual meeting.

2. **_Board Composition and Qualifications._** There are four positions on the Board, each role is defined specifically in a separate section of this agreement. All Board members must be Class A members of Pando.
    1. **_Class A Dissolution Impact._** If a Board member’s Class A membership is dissolved, that member also ceases to serve Pando as a Board member.

    2. **_Non-Occupant Member Requirement._** After the initial 3-week board of directors term has been completed, the number of directors that are Non-Occupant Class A Members may not represent less than 25% of the board unless:
        1. **_Absence of Non-Occupants._** There are no non-occupant Class A members.

        2. **_Temporary Waiver._** Non-occupant Class A members can unanimously vote to waive this requirement for one election term. A new vote is required for each subsequent term to continue the waiver.

3. **_Board Term._** Board members serve a term of two years, defined as from annual meeting to two (2) annual meetings later.

4. **_Resignation of Director._** A director may resign at any time by giving notice in a record to Pando. Unless the notice states a later effective date, a resignation is effective when the notice is received by Pando.

5. **_Removal of Director._** A Director serving on the Board may be removed at any time according to the following procedures:
    1. **_Cause._** Members may remove a director with or without cause.

    2. **_Proposal to Remove._** The Director shall be removed if a motion to remove the director is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class A members at any member meeting.

    3. **_Effective Date._** Removal is effective immediately after the motion is approved. The Director immediately loses all powers granted to them by this agreement.

6. **_Suspension of Director by Board._**
    1. **_Cause for Suspension._** A board of directors shall suspend a director if, considering the director’s course of conduct and the inadequacy of other available remedies, immediate suspension is necessary for the best interests of Pando.

    2. **_Board Approval._** A director is suspended if a motion to suspend a director is voted on and approved by a majority vote of the Board.

    3. **_Suspension Timeline._** A suspension under this subsection is effective for a period determined by the board of directors, not to exceed sixty (60) days, unless, before the end of the suspension period, the director is removed according to the removal process outlined in this agreement.

7. **_Vacancy on the Board._** A vacancy on the board of directors must be filled according to the procedures outlined below:
    1. **_Interim Board Member_**. The Board shall appoint an interim Director to serve on the Board.
       1. **_Timeframe._** The Board shall appoint an interim Director within a reasonable time.

       2. **_Board Approval._** The interim appointment shall be approved if a motion to appoint the interim Director is approved by majority vote of the remaining directors.

       3. **_Term Length._** The interim director serves until the next members meeting.

       4. **_Qualification and Board Composition._** The interim director must meet all qualifications and board composition requirements as outlined in this agreement.

    2. **_Replacement Board Member._** Class A members shall appoint a replacement Director.
       1. **_Timeframe._** Class A members shall appoint a replacement Director at the next members meeting.

       2. **_Class A Member Approval._** The replacement appointment shall be approved if a motion to appoint the replacement Director is approved by majority vote of the remaining directors.

       3. **_Term Length._** The replacement board member serves the remainder of the vacated position’s existing term.

       4. **_Qualification and Board Composition._** The replacement Director must meet all qualifications and board composition requirements as outlined in this agreement.

8. **_Board Decision-Making._** All Board actions require a simple majority vote of the Board to be approved. In the case of a tie, a Class A member vote will be called.

9. **_Board Empowerment._** Upon Board approval of an action, any Board member is authorized to execute that action on behalf of the Board, in accordance with the terms and conditions of the approval.

10. **_Roles and Responsibilities._** The board shall have the duties and obligations prescribed by the following:
    1. **_Management of Day-to-Day Affairs._** The Board shall oversee the management of Pando’s day-to-day operations, including any and all financial transactions involving amounts less than $2,000.

    2. **_Emergency Actions._** Emergency Actions: In the event of an emergent situation rendering a housing unit on Pando property uninhabitable, the Board is granted authority to act swiftly. The Board may allocate and expend up to $10,000 to implement an immediate solution to mitigate the loss for the property or occupant members..

    3. **_Execution of Projects._** The Board is empowered to manage and execute special projects in accordance with the project plans approved by a simple majority of Class A members. This includes making both budgetary and non-budgetary decisions within the parameters set forth in the project plan.

    4. **_Contractual Authority._** The Board is authorized to enter into, execute, and fulfill contracts, agreements, and other commitments binding Pando as necessary or appropriate to advance Pando’s objectives.

    5. **_Financial Management._** The Board is authorized to open and maintain bank accounts, investment accounts and other arrangements, draw checks and other orders for the payment of money, and designate individuals with authority to sign or give instructions with respect to those accounts and arrangements. Pando funds shall not be commingled with funds from other sources and shall be used solely for the business of Pando.

    7. **_Asset Management._** The Board is empowered to manage the acquisition utilization, maintenance, and disposition of Pando’s assets.

    8. **_Debt and Obligation Payment._** The Board is empowered to manage the payment of Pando’s debts and obligations, subject to the availability of funds. This does not include voluntary pre-payments.

    11. **_Tax Shelter Registration._** The Board is empowered to, if required, register Pando as a tax shelter and provide investor lists to the Secretary of the Treasury.

The Board has the power to enter into a nominee agreement with any such person, and such agreement may contain provisions indemnifying the nominee, except for their willful misconduct.

    14. **_Member-Directed Actions._** The Board is empowered to implement actions as directed by the Members in accordance with their approval of matters requiring member consent.

    15. **_General Conduct of Business._** The Board is empowered to undertake all necessary and appropriate actions and execute relevant documents in furtherance of Pando’s purposes and the conduct of its business.

## 6. Contributions, Profits, Losses, and Distributions

1. **_Additional Property Disclaimer._** All of section 6 assumes Pando owns one major piece of “real” property, located at [co-op property address] and other lower value “tangible” properties. If Pando wishes to purchase more “real” property, all Class A, B and C members should revisit this section and adjust the terms where necessary.

2. **_Pando Valuation Terms._** This section defines various terms used to define the value of Pando and determine the value of membership interest defined in the subsequent section.
   1. **_Initial Value of Pando Real Estate_** (`pando_re_value_initial`) is the initial purchase price of Pando Property, excluding closing costs. As of May 2024, this is equal to $1,299,000, based on the purchase of the property at [co-op property address].

   2. **_Value of Pando’s Real Estate_** (`pando_re_value`) is the most recent property appraisal or official sale price of [co-op property address], minus expected or actual liquidation costs (e.g., closing costs, appraisal costs, refinancing costs). Class A Members may make reasonable discretionary adjustments to this value to account for liquidation costs or unique circumstances that impact the property's value. Adjustments must be based on objective, verifiable factors, not arbitrary or unsupported, and approved by a 2/3rd vote of Class A Members before valuing and liquidating any membership unit. Class A Members must follow the general calculation method defined in this section and cannot determine Pando's Real Estate value without referring to the most recent appraisal or sale price.

   3. **_Value of Pando’s Tangible Assets_** (`pando_tang_value`) is the current fair market value of all tangible assets currently owned by Pando, as tracked by the Board. Class A Members may make reasonable discretionary adjustments to this value to account for unique circumstances or market conditions significantly impacting the assets' value. Adjustments must be based on objective, verifiable factors, not arbitrary or unsupported, and approved by a 2/3rd vote of Class A Members before valuing and liquidating any membership unit. Class A Members must adhere to the valuation method as fined by this section.

   4. **_Value of Pando’s Debts and Obligations_** (`pando_debt`) is the total current dollar amount Pando is legally obligated to pay any other individual or entity. This may include, but is not limited to outstanding mortgage and/or loan balances, unpaid property taxes, unpaid invoices, and unpaid fines or penalties levied against Pando. Class A Members may make reasonable discretionary adjustments to this value to account for unique circumstances. Adjustments must be based on objective, verifiable factors, not arbitrary or unsupported, and approved by a 2/3rd vote of Class A Members before valuing and liquidating any membership unit. Class A Members must adhere to the valuation method as fined by this section.

   5. **_Pando Real Estate Average Annual Appreciation Rate_** (`pando_re_ror`) is the annual rate at which Pando’s real estate has appreciated, rounded to the nearest hundredth of a percent (e.g. 3.66%). Class A Members may make reasonable discretionary adjustments to this value to account for unique circumstances or market conditions significantly impacting the property’s value. Adjustments must be based on objective, verifiable factors, not arbitrary or unsupported, and approved by a 2/3rd vote of Class A Members before valuing and liquidating any membership unit. Class A Members must adhere to the valuation method as fined by the following formula:

      ```text
      pando_re_ror = (pando_re_value / pando_re_value_initial)^(1 / ([Calendar Year of Liquidation Date] - 2024)) - 1
      ```

   6. **_Total Value of Pando_** (`pando_value`) is the current estimated value for Pando’s worth and is calculated based on the current market value of all assets owned by Pando, including real and tangible assets as well as any debts owed. The exact value shall be ratified and approved by a 2/3rd vote of Class A Members prior to valuing and liquidating any membership units and may include discretionary adjustments. The following formula describes the calculation method:

      ```text
      pando_value = pando_re_value + pando_tang_value - pando_debt
      ```

3. **_Contributions._** Members of Pando contribute financially towards the purchase of property. These contributions are converted into membership interests, classified as either preferred or common.
   1. **_Preferred Membership Interest._**
      1. **_Initial Contributions._** For every $1 contributed to Pando prior to February 1st, 2024 members received 1 preferred unit (i.e. 1 unit of preferred membership interest).
      2. **_Granting New Preferred Units._** New preferred membership units can be issued and granted to new or existing Class B members after a proposal to grant is approved by a 2/3rds vote of Class A members.
      3. **_Valuing Preferred Units._** The actual value in dollars of a preferred unit is determined based on the following:
         1. **_Preferred Grant Year_** (`p_grant_year`) is the calendar year in which the Preferred Membership Unit was granted. This must be defined for every unit of preferred membership interest, but may be defined in aggregate for a collection of preferred units.
         2. **_Preferred Minimum Rate of Return_** (`p_ror_min`) is the minimum annual growth rate that Pando is obligated to give to preferred units at liquidation events prior to giving any value to common units. This value is initially set to 3%.
         3. **_Preferred Maximum Rate of Return_** (`p_ror_max`) is the maximum annual growth rate that Pando will give to preferred units at liquidation events. This value is initially set to 8%.
         4. **_Preferred Actual Rate of Return_** (`p_ror_actual`) is the actual rate of return Pando will give to preferred units at the liquidation event. The actual rate of return is calculated as the minimum value between `p_ror_max` and the maximum value of `p_ror_min` and `pando_re_ror`, rounded to the nearest hundredth of a percent (e.g. 3.66%). In other words, it follows this formula:

            ```text
            p_ror_actual = MIN(p_ror_max, MAX(p_ror_min, pando_re_ror))
            ```

         5. **_Expected Preferred Unit Value_** (`p_value_expected`) is the current expected dollar value of a specific preferred unit and can be calculated as follows:

            ```text
            p_value_expected = (1 + p_ror_actual)^([Calendar Year of Liquidation Date] - p_grant_year)
            ```

         6. **_Total Expected Value of Preferred Membership Units_** (`total_p_value_expected`) is the sum of the Expected Preferred Unit Value across all preferred shares, as described by the following formula where `n` = the total number of all preferred membership units that have been granted:

            ```text
            total_p_value_expected = SUM(i = 1 to n, p_value_expected(i))
            ```

         7. **_Actual Preferred Unit Value_** (`p_value_actual`) is the actual dollar value of a specific preferred unit and is calculated based on the following:
            1. **_Bad Case._** If `pando_value` is less than `total_p_value_expected`, then the actual preferred unit value is calculated based on that unit’s relative proportion of the total expected value of preferred units as shown by the following formula:

               ```text
               p_value_actual = (p_value_expected / total_p_value_expected) * pando_value
               ```

            2. **_Expected Outcome._** If `pando_value` is greater than or equal to `total_p_value_expected`, then the actual preferred unit value is equal to the expected preferred unit value for that specific unit. In other words:

               ```text
               p_value_actual = p_value_expected
               ```

   2. **_Common Membership Interest._**
      1. **_Initial Contributions._** Initially, no common units are issued.
      2. **_Total Number of Common Units_** (`total_c_units`) is the total number of distinct common membership units that have been granted.
      3. **_Granting New Common Units._** New common membership units can be issued and granted to new or existing Class C members according to the following procedures:
         1. **_Automatic Granting for Monthly Contributions._** All Occupant Class A members are required to contribute towards monthly mortgage payments, property taxes, property insurance, and any other regular maintenance costs for Pando property as approved by a Class A member vote. This amount may be divided unequally among Occupant Class A members and/or any Non-Occupant Class A members who voluntarily elect to contribute. Each member is issued 1 common unit for each $1 contributed as part of their Monthly Contribution.
         2. **_Granting by Vote._** New common membership units can be issued and granted to new or existing Class C members after a proposal to grant is approved by a 2/3rds vote of Class A members.
      4. **_Common Unit Value_** (`c_value`) is the dollar value of a common unit and is determined according to the following:
         1. **_Bad Case._** If `pando_value` is less than or equal to `total_p_value_expected`, then the common unit value is equal to zero.
         2. **_Expected Outcome._** If `pando_value` is greater than `total_p_value_expected`, then common unit value is calculated based on that dividing the remaining value of Pando after deducting the total amount owed to preferred membership units (`total_p_value_expected`) evenly across all common units. In other words, it follows this calculation:

            ```text
            c_value = (pando_value - total_p_value_expected) / total_c_units
            ```

   3. **_Adjustments and Amendments._** All terms in this section may be adjusted by Pando. These adjustments will apply to all preferred and common units that have already been issued and any preferred and common units issued in the future. Amendments to this section follow the general amendment process defined elsewhere in this agreement and may not require a Class B or C membership vote.

4. **_Distributions._** Distributions to members shall occur solely at member liquidation events. This section outlines the conditions, processes, and calculations pertinent to such events.
   1. **_Member Liquidation Events._** A member liquidation event can be triggered under two primary conditions:
      1. **_Dissolution of Pando._** A vote to dissolve Pando is passed.
         1. **_Liquidation Date._** The official date of the liquidation event is the day the dissolution vote passes.
         2. **_Valuation of Membership Interest Units._** The value of preferred and common interests will be calculated after all of Pando’s assets have been liquidated and a new “Total Value of Pando” has been calculated.
         3. **_Delivery of Distribution._** The Board will issue a check to all Class B and C members in an amount equal to the total combined value of all their preferred and common units.
      2. **_Individual Member Decision._** A Class B or C member elects to liquidate their membership interest by submitting written notice via email to a board member, including a specified amount of preferred and common units they wish to liquidate.
         1. **_Liquidation Date._** The official date of the liquidation event is the day the member submits written notice of their intent to liquidate to a board member via email.
         2. **_Valuation of Membership Interest Units._** The value of preferred and common units will be determined after calculating an updated valuation for Pando (`pando_value`). Specifically, Pando will ensure an updated valuation by doing the following:
            1. **_Estimated or Actual Liquidation Costs._** Class A members will evaluate the cost of calculating values and liquidating funds to pay the Class B or C member out in order to include this in the updated value for Pando’s real estate (`pando_re_value`).
            2. **_Real Estate Appraisal._** Pando will immediately seek an official appraisal of Pando real estate property and use that to give an updated value for Pando’s real estate (`pando_re_value`).
            3. **_Updated Tangible Asset Value._** Class A members will audit existing records and determine the current total value of Pando’s tangible assets (`pando_tang_value`).
         3. **_Right of First Purchase._** Upon determination of unit values, all existing Class A members will be granted the right of first purchase for the common and preferred units being liquidated.
            1. **_Exercising._** Members may exercise this right by notifying the Board of their intent and specifying the quantity of units they wish to purchase.
            2. **_Multiple Members Interested._** If demand exceeds supply, the available units for purchase will be divided equally among interested Class A members, or in another mutually agreed upon manner.
         4. **_Purchase Plan._** Remaining unpurchased membership units will lead the Board to propose a purchase plan, potentially incorporating any combination of the following:
            1. **_Immediate Buyback._** Utilizing existing funds to purchase all or some of the membership units.
            2. **_Gradual Buyback._** Agreeing to purchase a fixed number of membership units over time at set intervals, including interest details.
            3. **_Refinanced Buyback._** Refinancing existing debt on Pando property to include the repurchase of the membership units.
            4. **_New Member Buyback:_** Seeking new members to make capital contributions in exchange for new membership units, subject to unanimous Class A member approval.
         5. **_Purchase Plan Entity Clarification._** In all scenarios, Pando is the purchasing entity, with the methods outlining capital sourcing.
         6. **_Purchase Timeline and Conditions._** Pando commits to a good faith effort to buy back membership interest units within a 4-year period, adhering to the following schedule:
            1. End of Year 1: 15% of total combined unit value purchased.
            2. End of Year 2: 40% of total combined unit value purchased.
            3. End of Year 3: 65% of total combined unit value purchased.
            4. End of Year 4: 100% of total combined unit value purchased.
         7. **_Timeline Extensions._** This timeline may be extended if adherence negatively impacts Pando's stability or necessitates liquidating over 20% of the “Total Value of Pando” in any calendar year, based on the most recent appraisal.
   2. **_Adjustments and Amendments._** This section is subject to amendments as per the general amendment process of the operating agreement and may not require a Class B or C membership vote.

5. **_Profits._** Pando does not intend to carry any profits, but may from time to time accrue interest on membership funds held for maintenance or other purposes. In these cases, profits will not be distributed back to members in any form.

6. **_Losses._**
   1. **_Definition and Recognition of Losses._** A loss is defined as any financial shortfall arising from Pando’s operations or property management. This includes, but is not limited to, operating deficits, depreciation in Pando property value, and expenses exceeding revenues.
   2. **_Losses on Mortgage Interest._** Losses attributed to mortgage interest will be passed on to members in proportion to the amount of mortgage interest they have paid.
   3. **_Member Liability for Losses._** In alignment with cooperative principles and Subchapter T regulations, members’ liability for the Co-op’s losses is limited to their patronage and capital contributions. No member shall bear personal liability beyond their engagement in the Co-op.

7. **_Transferability of Membership Interest._**
   1. **_Restriction on Transfer and Purchase._** Membership interests in Pando cannot be purchased or transferred by any entity or individual other than Pando, ensuring that control and ownership remain within the established cooperative structure.
   2. **_Exception in Case of Member's Death_**. In the event of a member's death, their membership interest will automatically transfer to their designated heir or as dictated by their estate plan.
   3. **_Continuation of Membership Interest Rights._** The heir receiving the membership interest assumes all associated responsibilities and rights as per the operating agreement.

8. **_Reimbursement._** Pando must reimburse the members for all direct out-of-pocket expenses incurred by them in managing Pando if those expenses are not considered contributions.

9. **_Adjustments and Amendments._** All terms in this section may be adjusted by Pando from time to time. These adjustments will apply to all preferred and common units that have already been issued and any preferred and common units issued in the future. Amendments to this section follow the general amendment process defined elsewhere in this agreement and may not require a Class B or C membership vote. By agreeing to this operating agreement, all members understand and agree that the terms in this section regarding contributions, profits, losses, and distributions may be revised as needed to maintain the financial health and stability of the company.

## 7. Voting Procedures

1. **_Voting Entities._** Every vote is conducted within a single, specific membership class (Class A, B, C) or subclass (Class A: Occupant, Non-Occupant) or within the appointed Board of Directors.
2. **_Initiation of Vote._** Any Class A member has the right to call for a vote on a specific issue or decision at any time.
3. **_Membership Voting._**
   1. **_Allocation._** Votes are allocated according to the rules of the specified membership class or subclass.
   2. **_Multi-Class Voting._** Members with memberships in multiple classes are entitled to vote in each class for which they are a member.
4. **_Board Voting Allocation._** Each Board member is entitled to one vote in Board-related matters.
5. **_Proxy and Delegate Voting._** Proxy and delegate voting are not permitted under any circumstances.
6. **_Vote Casting._** Members can cast votes affirmatively, negatively, or abstain. When multiple votes are allocated to an individual member, all of their votes must be cast in the same manner.
7. **_In-Meeting Voting._**
   1. **_Procedures._** The meeting and the vote must happen in accordance with established meeting protocols as specified elsewhere in the operating agreement.
   2. **_Voting Methodology._** Members present shall exercise their voting rights affirmatively, negatively, or via abstention.
   3. **_Voting Period._** The voting period for in-meeting votes shall conclude promptly after the vote is called and all votes for present members are counted.
   4. **_Majority Determination._** A majority is calculated based on the number of votes represented by members in attendance, specifically excluding abstentions unless explicitly stated otherwise in other sections of this document.
   5. **_Record Keeping._** All votes conducted during meetings will be meticulously recorded in the meeting minutes. This record will include the vote outcome and the voting tally.
8. **_Digital Voting._**
    1. **_Accepted Platforms._** Digital voting can be conducted through Pando’s designated Discord channel or via an email to Pando’s member email list.
    2. **_Notification Requirements._** Adequate notice shall be given through either:
       1. **_Discord Tags._** An “@everyone” tag in the specified Discord channel sent both at the start and one hour prior to the conclusion of the voting period.
       2. **_Emails._** Initial and reminder emails, sent both at the start and one hour prior to the conclusion of the voting period.
    3. **_Voting Methodology._** Members present shall exercise their voting rights affirmatively, negatively, or via explicit abstention.
    4. **_Vote Changes._** Members retain the right to change their vote at any point during the established voting period.
    5. **_Voting Period._**
       1. **_Standard Duration._** The default duration for digital voting is set to 24 hours unless an alternative duration is explicitly stated when calling the vote. This period should not be less than 24 hours unless there is a clear and present need for it to be less.
       2. **_Early Completion Clause._** The voting period may conclude early if all eligible voters have cast their votes at any point during the allotted time frame.
    6. **_Majority Determination._** Majority is calculated based on the total number of possible votes across the entire voting base, specifically excluding abstentions unless explicitly stated otherwise in other sections of this document.
    7. **_Record Keeping._** A comprehensive record of all digital votes will be maintained. This record, including vote outcomes and participation details, will be presented for review in the meeting minutes of the subsequent board or member meeting, ensuring full disclosure and member oversight of digital voting activities.
9. **_Voting Approval._** The vote is considered to be approved and the LLC shall take the proscribed action if at the end of the voting period the number of affirmative votes meets the majority requirement for the specific vote as defined by this agreement. Unless otherwise defined by this agreement, the majority requirement is a simple majority.
10. **_Effective Date._** The effective date for all votes is the moment the vote passes, unless a different date is explicitly specified in the voting action, in which case that date is the effective date.
11. **_Court Voting Determination._** The proper court may, upon application of Pando or any member who would be entitled to vote on the action at a members meeting, summarily state a record date for determining members entitled to sign records consenting to an action under this section and may enter other orders necessary or appropriate to effect the purposes of this section.

## 8. Meetings

1. **_Meeting Conduct._** Meetings will be conducted via Parliamentary Procedure utilizing Robert’s Rules of Order.
   1. **_Parliamentarian Appointment._** The chair may, at their own behest, appoint a parliamentarian to run procedural elements of meetings including but not limited to agenda management, timekeeping, rule enforcement, speaker recognition, voting procedures, points of question, and calls to order/adjournment.
2. **_Presiding Officer._** The Chair of the Board will be designated as the presiding officer of all member and Board meetings.
3. **_Meeting Location._** All member meetings may be held inside or outside of Colorado at the place selected by the Chair of the Board.
4. **_Meeting Participation._** Members may attend or conduct any member meeting through any means of communication if all members attending the meeting can communicate with each other during the meeting.
5. **_Board Meetings._**
   1. **_Meeting Dates._** Members shall meet at any time set by the Chair of the Board or when called from time to time by any other Board member.
   2. **_Closed Meetings._** Board meetings are considered closed such that only members of the Board are allowed to attend.
   3. **_Meeting Notice._** No notice is required for Board meetings and no agenda needs to be published beforehand.
   4. **_Meeting Agenda._** No agenda needs to be published before or after the meeting.
   5. **_Voting Documentation._** All votes taken during, and actions to be taken as a result of the outcomes, of any meeting of the board members must be summarized and publicized in the following ways:
      1. **_Discord Post._** A post in the board meetings channel of the Pando Discord Server with an @everyone tag must be made within 24 hours of the meeting adjournment
      2. **_Member Meeting Agenda._** A summary of all votes must be added to the meeting agenda for the next regular or annual member meeting.
   6. **_Quorum for Board Meetings._** Unless the operating agreement otherwise requires a different number of members or percentage of the voting power, a quorum for conducting business at all Board meetings consists of three (3) directors present at the meeting.
6. **_Member Meetings._**
   1. **_Meeting Agendas._** Every annual and regular meeting agenda shall include at a minimum:
      1. **_Meeting Minutes Review._** A review of the prior member meeting minutes and a vote for approval. Upon approval the secretary will transmit copies of the approved meeting minutes to all members by email via the group email and by uploading them to Pando’s shared digital file storage system.
      2. **_Financial Review._** A review of the current books of record including a summary of the budget and current membership interest for each member
   2. **_Annual Member Meetings._**
      1. **_Meeting Dates._** Members shall meet annually at a time set by the Chair of the Board.
      2. **_Additional Agenda Items._** The Board shall report, or cause to be reported, at Pando’s annual members meeting Pando’s business and financial condition as of the close of the most recent fiscal year, including a review of the current membership interest for each member.
      3. **_Impact of Failure to Meet._** Failure to hold an annual members meeting does not affect the validity of any action by Pando.
   3. **_Regular Member Meetings._** Members shall meet regularly (e.g. monthly) at intervals throughout the year between annual meetings, at times set by the Chair of the Board.
   4. **_Special Member Meetings._**
      1. **_Meeting Requirements._** A special meeting of members may be called only:
         1. **_Board Approval._** By a majority vote of the board of directors on a proposal stating the purpose of the meeting.
         2. **_By 10 Percent Member Demand._** By demand in a record submitted to the Secretary and signed by at least ten (10) percent of all members, regardless of membership class.
         3. **_Demand Withdrawal._** Any voting member may withdraw its demand for a special member meeting before receipt by Pando of demands sufficient to require a special meeting of members.
      2. **_Agenda Items._** Only business within the purpose or purposes stated in the notice of a special meeting of members may be conducted at the meeting.
   5. **_Notice of Member Meetings._**
      1. **_Notice Timeline._** The Board shall notify each member of the time, date, and place of a members meeting at least three (3) and not more than fourteen (14) days before the meeting for special and regular meetings, and not more than sixty (60) days before the meeting for annual meetings;
      2. **_Notice Agenda Requirements._**
         1. **_Annual Member Meetings._** Notice of an annual members meeting need not include any purpose of the meeting.
         2. **_Special Member Meetings._** Notice of a special meeting of members shall include each purpose of the meeting as contained in the demand for the meeting or as voted upon by the Board.
         3. **_Regular Member Meetings._** Notice of a regular meeting shall include the initial proposed agenda for the meeting.
      3. **_Mode of Notice._** Notice for all member meetings shall be provided via either of the following options;
         1. **_Email._** An email to Pando’s member email list.
         2. **_Discord._** A post in Pando’s Discord channel using the “@everyone” tag.
   6. **_Waiver of Members Meeting Notice_**
      1. A member may waive notice of a members meeting before, during, or after the meeting.
      2. A member’s participation in a members meeting is a waiver of notice of that meeting unless the member objects to the meeting at the beginning of the meeting or promptly upon the member’s arrival at the meeting and does not thereafter vote for or assent to action taken at the meeting.
   7. **_Quorum for Member Meetings._** Unless the operating agreement otherwise requires a different number of members or percentage of the voting power, a quorum for conducting business at all meetings of the members consists of fifty-one (51) percent of the total number of Class A members or five (5) members present at the meeting, whichever is less.

## 9. Purchase of Real Estate Property

1. **_Decision to Purchase._** Real estate property may be purchased by Pando at any time according to the procedure outlined below:
   1. **_Proposal to Purchase._** A member shall submit a proposal to purchase that may include:
      1. **_The Property’s Address._**
      2. **_The Offer Price._**
      3. **_Financing Details._** This shall include:
         1. **_Down Payment Amount._**
         2. **_Loan Amount._**
         3. **_Loan Details._** This may include the bank or other financial institution granting the loan, the interest rate, the loan term, and an identification of any personal guarantees that will be required by members to obtain the loan.
         4. **_Closing Costs._** An estimate of additional closing costs that will be required to complete the purchase.
      4. **_Renovation Details._** A description and estimated cost of any immediate renovations, repairs, or maintenance that will be required post-purchase.
      5. **_Disclosures._** A disclosure of any known relationships between the seller and any members of Pando.
      6. **_Insurance._** Preliminary details or estimates on the type and cost of insurance required for the property.
   2. **_Class A Member Approval._** A proposal to purchase is voted on and approved unanimously by all Class A members.
   3. **_Class B Member Approval._** A proposal to purchase is voted on and approved by a 2/3rds majority of Class B members. If no Class B members exist, this vote is not necessary.
   4. **_Class C Member Approval._** A proposal to purchase is voted on and approved by a 2/3rds majority of Class C members. If no Class C members exist, this vote is not necessary.
   5. **_Vote Order._** The Class A member vote shall occur first. If it does not pass, no other member votes shall be called.

2. **_Under Contract Phase._** Upon receiving initial approval and subsequent acceptance of the purchase offer by the property seller, the proposal will move to the “Under Contract Phase.” During this phase, a unanimous vote of all Class A members is required to move past all key objection deadlines including, but not limited to the following:
   1. **_Inspection._**
   2. **_Appraisal._**
   3. **_Financing._**
3. **_Compliance with Laws and Regulations._** All property purchasing activities will be conducted in full compliance with relevant local, state, and federal laws and regulations.

## 10. Sale of Real Estate Property

1. **_Decision to Sell._** Any real estate property owned by Pando may be sold for any reason at any time according to the procedure outlined below:
   1. **_Proposal to Sell._** A member must submit a detailed proposal to sell to the Board outlining reasons for sale, price range, and expected sale timeline.
   2. **_Class A Member Approval._** A proposal to sell is voted on and approved by a 3/4ths majority of Class A members.
   3. **_Class B Member Approval._** A proposal to sell is voted on and approved by a 3/4ths majority of Class B members.
   4. **_Class C Member Approval._** A proposal to sell is voted on and approved by a 3/4ths majority of Class C members.
   5. **_Vote Order._** The Class A member vote shall occur first. If it does not pass, no other member votes shall be called.
2. **_Right of First Offer for Members._**
   1. **_Exercise._** A member wishing to exercise their right of first offer, must submit a legally binding formal offer to purchase the Pando property to the board within three weeks of the approval of the proposal to sell.
   2. **_Public Listing Delay._** The Board will not publicly list the Pando property until the “Right of First Offer” exercise window has expired.
   3. **_Offer Requirements._** The purchase offer should comply with all local laws and regulations and should include all standard and legally required components of a purchase offer.
   4. **_Offer Acceptance._** The acceptance of the purchase offer from a member shall follow the same decision-making process as used for purchase offers from nonmembers as defined elsewhere in this agreement.
3. **_Development of Sale Management Plan._** The Board is responsible for developing a comprehensive plan for managing the Pando property sale within a month of finalizing the decision to sell. The sale management plan must include the following, though certain sections may be null initially:
   1. **_List Price._**
   2. **_Sale Preparations._** A listing of all proposed repairs, renovations, and potential staging updates to be made prior to listing the Pando property for sale.
   3. **_Marketing Strategies._** The delineation of marketing strategies aimed at attracting potential buyers.
   4. **_Showing Procedures._** Clearly defined procedures for how Pando property showings will be scheduled and how members residing in the Pando property will be notified.
   5. **_Agent Selection._** The selection and identification of the real estate agent or agency that will be entrusted with overseeing and managing the transaction.
   6. **_Plan Amendment._** A process that will be followed when updating and amending the Sale Management Plan.
4. **_Approval of Sale Management Plan._** The Sale Management Plan must be approved by a majority vote of Class A voters.
5. **_Offer Acceptance._** An offer to purchase contract from a potential buyer shall be accepted according to the following procedure:
   1. **_Class A Member Approval._** A proposal to accept the offer is voted on and approved by a 3/4ths majority of Class A members.
   2. **_Class B Member Approval._** A proposal to accept the offer is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class B members.
   3. **_Class C Member Approval._** A proposal to accept the offer is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class B members.
   4. **_Vote Order._** The Class A member vote shall occur first. If it does not pass, no other member votes shall be called.
   5. **_Notification of Rejection._** Should the Board reject a purchase offer, they are obligated to notify all members of their decision within 24 hours.
6. **_Post-Acceptance Sale Process._** After the acceptance of a purchase offer, the following shall occur:
   1. **_Legal Compliance._** The Pando property sale shall adhere to all standard and legally mandated processes for real estate transactions in the state of Colorado.
   2. **_Offer Contract Amendments._**
      1. **_Minor Amendments._** The Board may act according to their established roles and responsibilities to accept any offer contract amendments with a financial impact on Pando that is less than or equal to $10,000.
      2. **_Major Amendments._** Any offer contract amendments with a financial impact on Pando that is greater than $10,000 shall be subject to the same decision-making process as outlined in 10.5.
7. **_Conditions for Sale._** The real estate property shall only be considered eligible for sale if it has been owned by Pando for a minimum of one year and will not be sold at a loss, unless the proposal to sell is approved by a unanimous vote of Class A members.
8. **_Sale Proceeds._** All proceeds from the sale of the Pando property shall be owned by Pando.
9. **_Timelines and Procedures._** The board is empowered to establish additional timelines or procedures for the sale. Any such procedures or requirements should be in harmony with the provisions of this agreement and must adhere to any and all applicable laws and regulations.
10. **_Sale Updates._** After the Pando property has been officially listed for sale, the board is responsible for providing written weekly updates to both members and shareholders. These updates will detail the status of the sale and will continue until the completion and closure of a purchase deal.

## 11. Dissolution

1. **_Dissolution Decision._** Pando may be dissolved at any time following the procedure outlined below:
   1. **_Class A Member Approval._** A dissolution proposal is voted on and approved by a 2/3rds majority of Class A members.
   2. **_Class B Member Approval._** A dissolution proposal is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class B members.
   3. **_Class C Member Approval._** A dissolution proposal is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class C members.
   4. **_Vote Order._** The Class A vote shall occur first. If it does not pass, no other member votes shall be called.
2. **_Execution of Dissolution._** Post-decision, the Board is responsible for executing the dissolution, encompassing asset liquidation, debt settlement, and equitable asset distribution including, but not limited to the following:
   1. **_Real Estate Liquidation._** Liquidation of real estate assets owned by Pando shall be conducted as per the process in Section 10.
   2. **Other Asset Liquidation.** To liquidate all non-real estate assets, the Board shall follow the process outlined below:
      1. **_Catalog Preparation._** The Board shall compile a comprehensive catalog of all non-real estate assets owned by Pando complete with descriptions, condition, and estimated market value.
      2. **_Member Purchase Right._** The Board will give all members the right of first purchase of non-real estate assets according to the following process:
         1. **_Catalog Distribution._** The Board distributes the catalog to all members for review.
         2. **_Purchase Intent._** Members will have a one-week period to indicate interest in purchasing items by informing any member of the Board.
         3. **_Purchaser Selection Process._** If multiple members show interest in the same item, the Board will determine the buyer through a randomized method, avoiding any and all bidding wars.
         4. **_Purchase._** After one week's time has passed, members who expressed a purchase intent may purchase items at their specified market value.
      3. **_Public Sale._** Any items not purchased by members will subsequently be listed for public sale on platforms like Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, or equivalent marketplaces. Board members may sell the assets at any price they deem acceptable.
      4. **_Donation of Unsold Items._** Items that remain unsold by the time of the surrendering of the articles of incorporation will be donated to a local thrift store or charity.
   3. **_Final Asset Distribution._** Any residual assets, after debt clearance, shall be disseminated to members proportionately, based on their preferred and common membership interest.
   4. **_Formal Dissolution._** The dissolution concludes with the surrender of the Articles of Incorporation to the Colorado Secretary of State.
   5. **_Dissolution Timeline._** There are no specific timelines or additional procedures for dissolution. However, the Board may establish additional procedures or requirements for the dissolution, subject to the provisions of this agreement and any applicable laws and regulations.

## 12. Amendments to This Agreement

1. **_Amendment Approvals._** This agreement may be amended at any time according to the following procedure:
   1. **_Class A Member Approval._** A proposed amendment is voted on and approved by a 3/4ths majority of Class A members. Any abstention will be counted as a nay vote.
   2. **_Class B Member Approval._**
      1. **_Class B Member Approval._** A proposed amendment is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class B members. Any abstention will be counted as a nay vote.
      2. **_Class B Approval Exemption._** Class B Member approval is NOT required to amend this agreement if the amendment does not relate to Pando dissolution, the purchase of new real estate property, or the sale of real estate property owned by Pando.
   3. **_Class C Member Approval._**
      1. **_Class C Member Approval._** A proposed amendment is voted on and approved by a simple majority of Class C members. Any abstention will be counted as a nay vote.
      2. **_Class C Approval Exemption._** Class C Member approval is NOT required to amend this agreement if the amendment does not relate to Pando dissolution, the purchase of new real estate property, or the sale of real estate property owned by Pando.
   4. **_Vote Order._** The Class A vote shall occur first. If it does not pass, no other member votes shall be called.
2. **_Effective Date._** Upon the successful approval of an amendment according to the above procedures, the amendment shall take effect immediately unless otherwise specified in the amendment proposal.
3. **_Additional Procedures._** The Board may establish additional non-voting procedures or requirements for the amendment process, subject to the provisions of this article and any applicable laws and regulations.

## 13. Indemnification

1. **_General Indemnification Clause._** Pando will indemnify any person who was or is a party defendant or is threatened to be made a party defendant, in a pending or completed action, suit or proceeding, whether civil, criminal, administrative, or investigative (other than an action by or in the right of Pando) by reason of the fact that the person is or was a member of Pando, employee, or agent of Pando , or is or was serving at the request of Pando, for instant expenses (including attorney's fees), judgments, fines, and amounts paid in settlement actually and reasonably incurred in connection with such action, suit or proceeding if the members determine that the person acted in good faith and in a manner he or she reasonably believed to be in or not opposed to the best interest of Pando, and with respect to any criminal action proceeding, has no reasonable cause to believe his or her conduct was unlawful. The termination of any action, suit, or proceeding by judgment, order, settlement, conviction, or upon a plea of "no lo Contendere" or its equivalent, does not in itself create a presumption that the person did or did not act in good faith and in a manner which he or she reasonably believed to be in the best interest of Pando, and, with respect to any criminal action or proceeding, had reasonable cause to believe that his or her conduct was lawful.
