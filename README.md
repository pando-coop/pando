# Pando Collective Docs

This repository is a public collection of documents, templates, and processes from Pando Collective LLC, a housing co-op in Colorado. We are sharing these materials for other groups who are exploring cooperative housing, member-owned real estate, or similar collective ownership structures.

These documents are not a perfect blueprint. They are a record of what we have used, what we are learning from, and what may be useful to adapt.

For an introduction to our co-op, watch our [Retrofit Community Case Study: Pando Collective](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJHpmOt9qr8) presentation from the 2026 Community Rooted Housing Summit in Denver, CO.

## How To Use This

You are welcome to read, copy, remix, and adapt these documents for your own organizing work. A few suggestions:

1. Treat the operating agreement as a conversation starter, not a plug-and-play legal document.
2. Pay close attention to where your group’s needs differ from ours: membership classes, voting thresholds, financing, tax treatment, member exits, property ownership, and dispute resolution.
3. Work with a lawyer, accountant, or other qualified professional before relying on anything here for an actual entity, property purchase, fundraising round, or tax position.
4. Keep a version history of your own changes. Cooperative governance docs are living documents, and it helps to know why a clause changed.

## Notes For Other Co-ops

Some topics in these documents are especially worth discussing early:

- How members join, leave, or change occupancy status
- How capital contributions become ownership or membership interests
- How the co-op values member interests when someone needs to cash out
- What decisions require unanimous approval versus majority or supermajority approval
- What the board can do on its own, and what requires a member vote
- How meeting minutes, financial records, and shared documents are maintained
